About Incept Course Builder

One local workspace for building Alpha courses with native tools, selected agents, and evidence you can inspect.

Start from a course idea or an existing repository. The installed builder gives you a customized local dashboard that updates as your agent saves progress: blueprint, content generation, quality checks, assembly and publication. The public Courses page shows shared examples; your course workspace stays on your Mac.

Native tools do the work

Content Factory, AP One, and TimeBack remain the authoritative course systems. The builder brings together their existing course workflows, tools, and verification requirements. Align, Synthesize, Assemble, and Prove organize the view; each course retains its native process.

Your agent, your workspace

Choose Codex, Claude Code, Hermes, or another local agent with filesystem and terminal access. Skills supply the task-specific instructions and resources. The builder loads the relevant workflow as needed; service credentials and model access remain separate.

Coverage before completion

Required content populations have separate acceptance, integration, and served evidence. A passing bank proves its own scope. Missing measurements stay UNMEASURED, and a published position retains its source date.

Public status, private work

This site displays published observations and documentation. The private package requires authorized Alpha/Incept GitHub access. Course content, local checkpoints, credentials, and private runbook resources belong in the authorized workspace.

Start on your Mac

Open Terminal and run the installer:

curl -fsSL https://joshuadurey-del.github.io/incept-course-builder/install.sh | bash
  1. Sign in to GitHub. Use an account with a verified @alpha.school email and access to the private InceptTrilogy package.
  2. Name your course and choose an agent. Setup checks the available local tools. Native services and selected workflows may require their own runtime and authorization.
  3. Scan, then continue. Review the remaining population coverage and the next eligible action before course execution.
~/.local/bin/incept-course-builder scan
~/.local/bin/incept-course-builder build

Open the local dashboard later with ~/.local/bin/incept-course-builder. The dashboard stays available after Terminal closes. Run incept-course-builder stop to stop it. Re-run the install command to update. Model subscriptions are separate.

Automation with clear authority

Installing the builder or signing in does not authorize course publication or spending. Native checks, recorded permissions, and course-specific ownership still apply. Required human walkthroughs and release decisions remain part of acceptance.

The public site is a read-only view. It does not execute local commands or launch a course.
