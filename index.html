Overview — all four courses

All four courses now use one runbook-aligned lifecycle. HumGeo is at Gate 4 profile/authority work; APWH remains at Gate 2 with nine unknown blueprint clauses; APUSH and Psychology retain prior evidence while their current baselines are established.

Sprint target

LS REVIEW READY

The milestone is simple: Learning Science can open the named course and walk it. The handoff supplies a working URL, access, the candidate version, a short representative route, and any known defect that could block or mislead the review. It is not LS approval or release.

Native TimeBack route: Ilma's runbook now supplies the publication sequence. A candidate is not reviewer-visible merely because the route is known: profile, graph/preview, sealed all-absent plan, dark publication, canary enrollment, private activation, exact readbacks, and zero-write replays must close in order.

Blueprint coverage and QC provenance remain internal build controls. They do not substitute for a course-specific closure gate or become an intake process imposed on Learning Science. Existing generation QC and grader calibration are reused rather than repeated; HumGeo does not use an 80% counter to select work.

Work evidence and gate credit stay separate. Course pages file existing artifacts under the gate where they most likely belong. Only a course-approved closure receipt changes a gate state.

What earns LS REVIEW READY

  • Give Learning Science a working URL and the required access.
  • Pin the candidate version they are seeing.
  • Verify a representative course → unit → lesson → practice → gate path.
  • Name defects that could affect the walkthrough and the feedback owner.

What comes after

  • Learning Science applies its own review process and records findings.
  • Evidence-backed revision and course-specific specialist disposition.
  • Production completeness, deployment/readback, registration, and learner walk.
  • Release only after the exact served bytes pass the required end-to-end checks.
