Overview — all four courses

Current work to produce pinned, locally validated course candidates. External Learning Science access is held until the sanctioned TimeBack publishing instructions arrive; focused-work ranges exclude that wait.

Sprint target

LS REVIEW READY

The milestone is simple: Learning Science can open the named course and walk it. The handoff supplies a working URL, access, the candidate version, a short representative route, and any known defect that could block or mislead the review. It is not LS approval or release.

External hold — TimeBack instructions: no current candidate may be published, registered, placed in a chooser, or described as reviewer-visible until the sanctioned TimeBack route is supplied and the exact deployed version is read back. Builders continue local validation and package preparation in parallel.

Blueprint coverage and QC provenance remain internal build controls. The roughly 80% blueprint target helps prioritize work, but it is not an intake process imposed on Learning Science. Existing generation QC and grader calibration are reused rather than repeated.

The four numbered stages are sprint-planning terms. Course blueprints remain the design authority; current factory contracts govern execution. Course markers are current recovery loci and never prove that every earlier factory phase cleared canonically.

What earns LS REVIEW READY

  • Give Learning Science a working URL and the required access.
  • Pin the candidate version they are seeing.
  • Verify a representative course → unit → lesson → practice → gate path.
  • Name defects that could affect the walkthrough and the feedback owner.

What comes after

  • Learning Science applies its own review process and records findings.
  • Evidence-backed revision and course-specific specialist disposition.
  • Production completeness, deployment/readback, registration, and learner walk.
  • Release only after the exact served bytes pass the required end-to-end checks.
