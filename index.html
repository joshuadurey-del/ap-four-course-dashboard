Sprint target

LS-QC READY at approximately 80% blueprint match

The milestone is a Learning Scientist review candidate—not a released course. Owner direction sets the handoff bar at roughly 80% blueprint match with the exact candidate, measured denominator, generation/QC provenance, known gaps, a reviewer-visible surface, and no known answer-key exposure, placeholder, or critical broken review path.

Existing factory QC and grader calibration count. Questions already QC checked during generation do not need a duplicate blanket pass, April grader calibration is not reopened by empty local folders, and unnamed human signoff is not required before sharing with Learning Science.

The four numbered stages are sprint-planning terms. Course blueprints remain the design authority; current factory contracts govern execution. Course markers are current recovery loci and never prove that every earlier factory phase cleared canonically.

What earns LS-QC READY

  • Pin the blueprint/CED, candidate version, and honest review denominator.
  • Measure current coverage and bind inherited generation/QC provenance.
  • Record every known gap instead of silently shrinking the denominator.
  • Stage the exact candidate for immediate human review and verify the critical review path.
  • Seal the handoff packet and name Learning Science as the next owner.

What comes after

  • Named human Learning Scientist review and findings.
  • Evidence-backed revision and course-specific specialist disposition.
  • Production completeness, deployment/readback, registration, and learner walk.
  • Release only after the exact served bytes pass the required end-to-end checks.
