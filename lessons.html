An honest accounting of last week

Lesson learnedApplied going forward
We generated a whole corpus without real QC.The factory now judges every item inside generation. APWH holds strict repair-first, judge-once: 449 repairs applied, zero paid verdicts spent. Every HumGeo rebuild pass binds to the item's exact bytes.
Sealed plans can go stale against their own sources.Plans now take receipted amendments as first-class input, with a sandbox proof required before execution: 425 prior passes intact, 49 slots selected.
We measured our shortcut before trusting it, and it failed.A local pre-check judge missed 1 in 5 real failures in a 105-call test. It never gated anything and is now off entirely. Nothing gets judging authority without measurement.
Both deadlines slipped about a day, for a structural reason.Four courses share one factory pool in strict series. The chain is now automated: each lane's close writes a receipt and the next lane fires on it.
Work sat parked that was executable immediately, twice.Standing rule: everything buildable builds now; only the truly gated command waits. The 30-slot amendment was fully built and proven while its blocker still ran.
Corrective waves need semantic proof, not just structural success.Our seal requires a byte-bound official pass per record plus a fingerprint manifest anyone can re-check.
